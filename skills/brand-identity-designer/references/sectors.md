# Sector codes and clichés

Format: **Sector**: what the audience needs to feel · proof to show · colour temperament seen in the category · typical type · logo clichés to avoid → a better source of ideas.
Category codes are trust signals: keep them in the proof layer, break them in the visual layer only with a reason. Before building, also run the general clichés and model defaults in `routes.md`. If a client spans two sectors, the one driving most revenue is primary.

## Technology and professional
- **B2B SaaS:** confidence · real product UI, named customer results, a security page people can forward · cool neutrals + one saturated hue (crowded with indigo) · clean grotesk, tabular figures · globe, circuit, brain, sparkle, hexagons, nodes → a specific idea about what the product does for people; the symbol must work as an app icon from day one.
- **AI products:** grounded curiosity · real unedited outputs, a plain data policy · graphite + one signal colour, or warm paper · geometric sans + mono · four-point sparkle, brain, orb, blue-to-purple gradient → what the product actually changes in a person's work.
- **Developer tools:** competence, speed · copyable code, a working quick start · dark with syntax hues or crisp light · mono + humanist sans · brackets and terminal prompts as the whole idea.
- **Cybersecurity:** calm control · independent evaluations, dated certifications · dark slate + one alert colour · technical sans + mono · shields, padlocks, keyholes.
- **Fintech and banking:** safety, control · regulatory lines beside claims, full fee tables · deep green or ink on warm white · sturdy sans with tabular figures · upward arrow, bar chart, coin, pillars → order and clarity through proportion and spacing.
- **Accounting and insurance:** orderly relief · credentials by number, visible package prices · ledger green or terracotta on light · plain grotesk · same clichés as fintech, plus umbrellas.
- **Consulting and professional services:** respect for expertise · named partners, published thinking · ink and paper, one restrained accent · editorial serif + neutral sans · abstract swoosh, interlocking rings, mountain peak → the firm's method or name drawn with intent.
- **Law:** reassurance, resolve · attorney faces and admissions, fee terms · oxblood or navy on warm stone (navy + gold is the model default) · classic serif or bold semi-condensed · scales, columns, gavel → typographic authority: a well-drawn serif wordmark or monogram.
- **Creative agencies:** admiration · case studies that show thinking · one loud colour or off-white · judged by decks, case studies and LinkedIn more than the logo.

## Health and care
- **Hospitals and clinics:** calm, safety · find-a-doctor, insurance by plan, accreditation · cool teal or deep green on white · highly legible humanist sans · **red cross (legally protected under the Geneva Conventions)**, heartbeat line, caduceus (often confused with the Rod of Asclepius) → care philosophy or place through type and colour. Risk: sterile and forgettable without real faces.
- **Dental:** ease for nervous patients · the dentist's own face, insurance list, prices · blue-green on white is the code; terracotta on linen breaks it · friendly geometric sans or soft serif · tooth outline, smiling tooth, toothbrush → a form from the founder's name, a calm abstract shape, warm type with one memorable detail; off-white "bone" instead of clinical white.
- **Mental health:** safety, gentleness · therapist profiles with licences, fees, a calm crisis line · muted sage and warm neutrals · rounded sans or quiet serif · head silhouettes, puzzle pieces, lotus; use illustration or non-identifying photos for sensitive subjects.
- **Telehealth and pharmacy:** relief, discretion · prescriber licensing, verifiable pharmacy credentials · clean blue or deep plum on warm neutral · clear sans · pill capsules, crosses.
- **Fitness:** energy, belonging · published prices, live timetable, real members · black + volt, or clay + sage · condensed display or soft serif · flexing arm, dumbbell, running figure → energy through type, angle and colour. Volt and orange fail contrast on light grounds.
- **Spa, salon, beauty:** indulgence, care · own-work gallery, priced menu · blush + cream or black + one pop · fine serif or bold geometric · lotus, faces in profile, scissors.

## Home and local services (vehicles and phones are the main media)
- **HVAC:** relief, reliability · licence numbers, published fees, named technicians · navy + safety orange, or teal on warm white · bold semi-condensed sans · snowflake + flame, thermometer, red/blue split, mountain peaks, stripes under a roof → the comfort outcome, airflow as rhythm, trade schematic symbols, a strong wordmark built for van sides and rear doors; the phone number as a design element.
- **Plumbing, electrical:** reassurance under pressure · licences, call-out fee, workmanship guarantee · royal blue + yellow or graphite + copper · sturdy rounded sans · water drop, wrench, pipe-as-letter / lightning bolt, bulb, plug → the promise (first-visit fix, upfront price) carried by type and colour; a line or path drawn with precision.
- **Roofing and construction:** security, pride · local project gallery, manufacturer certifications (official badge files only), a physical address · charcoal + brick or cream + forest · condensed or slab · chevron roof over the name → materials, an abstracted shingle pattern, a confident wordmark.
- **Landscaping and lawn:** pride in the yard · portfolio, priced plans · grass green on white or moss + stone · leaf, tree, mower, grass blades → seasonal rhythm, cut-lawn stripes, a planting grid, land contours.
- **Pool builders:** aspiration, family joy · deep gallery, build timeline, price ranges · pool blue + sun, or stone + charcoal · wave, drop, palm → tile geometry, a signature pool outline, light on water as pattern.
- **Cleaning:** peace of mind · instant price, stated vetting, re-clean guarantee · teal + mint or navy + brass · sparkles, bubbles, spray bottle → order and calm through colour and space.
- **Pet services:** trust, affection · staff handling real pets, prices by breed, safety policies · warm white + violet or tangerine, or calm teal · rounded friendly sans · paw print, bone, heart with paw → a characterful letterform, a person-animal gesture; the mobile van is often the main ad.
- **Auto:** honesty, competence · certified technicians, a price menu · charcoal + signal red or white + brand blue · condensed technical sans · checkered flags, pistons, wings.

## Places, food and retail
- **Restaurants and cafes:** appetite, welcome · real menu with prices, accurate hours, real dishes · warm paper + tomato or black + gold · warm serif · chef's hat, crossed fork and knife → cuisine culture, the room, founder story, distinctive lettering.
- **Food and beverage packaging:** craving, provenance · store locator, nutrition, sourcing · plan regulatory panels first (ingredients, nutrition, warnings, barcode, recycling marks), then brand the rest.
- **Hotels and hospitality:** anticipation, ease · live rates, true room photos · sand + sea or charcoal + brass · elegant serif + clean sans.
- **Travel and tourism:** wanderlust with confidence · day-by-day itineraries, licensing and financial protection · earth tones or refined neutrals · globes, planes, palm trees, compass roses.
- **Real estate:** possibility, local confidence · recent local sales, agent licences, honest photos · deep green on white or black + ivory · house roof, keys, roofline peaks → a letterform with architectural detail, an abstracted local landmark. Development renders labelled "indicative".
- **Architecture and interiors:** taste, assurance · credited portfolio, registration, fee approach · white and black or plaster + terracotta · restrained grotesk or characterful serif.
- **Fashion and DTC:** desire · fit data, returns, customer photos · ink + white or one saturated hue · small precise sans or condensed display; wordmark first.
- **Skincare and beauty DTC:** confidence in the formula · full ingredient list, unretouched results · clinical blue on white or sand + olive.
- **Luxury:** rarity · craft detail, provenance · ivory + black or deep colour fields · high-contrast serif · dark + gold as automatic premium is a default, not a strategy.

## Public, social and culture
- **Education:** ambition, belonging · accreditation, sourced outcomes, full cost · heritage maroon + gold or bright blue + orange · books, mortarboards, lamps.
- **Nonprofits:** empathy turning into action · registration numbers, financials, dignified stories · deep green + rust or saturated red · hands holding a heart, figures in a circle → the specific change the organisation makes; a combination mark that reproduces cheaply in one colour.
- **Government and civic:** clarity, fairness · official domain, plain language, accessibility statement · white and black + one official colour · never imitate seals or coats of arms.
- **Events and weddings:** excitement, romance · real event photos, priced packages · blush + sage or black + a hot colour · a dynamic identity or strong wordmark with variable colour.
- **Media and publishing:** curiosity and trust · bylines, dates, corrections policy · newsprint + one red.
- **Faith and community:** welcome · times and address up front, named leaders, safeguarding policy.
- **Personal brands:** the person is the product; the photography direction is the highest-leverage asset (first impressions of a face form in about 100 ms, Todorov & Willis 2006); a wordmark or monogram plus a strict template system; one avatar everywhere.

## Global tropes to avoid by default
Blue-to-purple "AI" gradients · dark + gold as automatic premium · stock handshake and laptop-pointing photos · grey text on colour failing contrast · an accent-coloured "magic word" in the headline · a row of unverified big numbers · hero photos washed out under heavy overlays · style words leaking into visible copy.
