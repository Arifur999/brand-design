# Industry notes for interfaces

Read only the client's industry. Format: **Industry** · what earns trust on screen · two direction options · motion level · must-haves · clichés and pitfalls. Category conventions are trust signals: keep them in the proof layer and differentiate in the visual layer. For logo and brand-level sector clichés, see the brand skill.

Choosing a direction: start from the audience's emotional need, not the client's taste. Urgent, price-led or broad audiences → direct, trustworthy, warm directions. Planned, experience-led or premium purchases → editorial, quiet-luxury or dark-premium directions. Pick one direction and borrow at most one signature move from a second.

## Business and technology
- **B2B SaaS** · real product UI, named results, a forwardable security page · precise neutral / warm editorial · medium · two CTAs (trial vs demo), interactive tour, demo form ≤ 5 fields · category-describing hero ("all-in-one platform"), fake dashboards, pricing hidden behind "Contact us".
- **AI products** · real unedited outputs, plain data policy · instrument panel / human paper · medium · try one real task before sign-up, streaming progress, edit and regenerate · blue-purple gradients and sparkles, "99% accurate" without a method, cursor-typing videos.
- **Fintech and banking** · regulator lines beside claims, full fee tables · solid and calm / everyday friendly · low · rates with "as of" date and conditions in the same block, a calculator using the user's numbers · neon crypto styling for savings products, fees in PDFs.
- **Developer tools** · copyable code, a quick start that works first time · terminal-native dark / docs-first light · low · copy buttons, remembered language choice, self-serve API keys, Ctrl/Cmd+K · no code above the fold, code as images, gated docs.
- **Cybersecurity** · independent evaluations, dated certifications, a trust centre · calm command / clear assurance · low · demo and sandbox, trust-centre downloads · fear copy and red everywhere, certifications in progress listed as done.
- **Consulting** · named partners, published thinking · quiet authority / modern operator · low · jargon headlines, initials-only teams, case studies without results.
- **Law** · attorney faces and admissions, fee terms, results with disclaimers · steady counsel / direct advocate · low · sticky click-to-call, 4-field intake · gavels, scales, columns, settlement figures without disclaimers.
- **Accounting and insurance** · credentials by number, visible package prices · orderly ledger / neighbourly advisor · low · handshakes over paperwork, no prices.

## Health and wellness
- **Hospitals and clinics** · find-a-doctor, insurance by plan, accreditation · clinical calm / community care · low · symptom-aware search ("knee pain"), booking or callback with SMS confirmation, call and directions in one tap · buried patient portal, jargon navigation, hero carousels.
- **Dental** · the dentist's own face, insurance list, treatment prices · fresh precision / warm boutique · low · live-availability booking, sticky Call + Book, an emergency path · stock smiling models, graphic clinical photos, hidden elective prices.
- **Mental health** · licensed therapist profiles, fees, a calm crisis line · soft shelter / grounded clarity · low · matching quiz leading to a real shortlist, booking without an account, skippable sensitive questions · alarm-red crisis banners, credential walls before warmth, scarcity tactics.
- **Fitness** · published prices, live timetable, real members · high energy / calm studio · high · two-tap class booking, intro offer, membership table · prices hidden to force tours, elite-athlete-only imagery, autoplay sound; volt and orange fail as text on light grounds.
- **Spa, salon, beauty** · own-work gallery, priced menu, stylist profiles · soft luxe / bold studio · medium · PDF menus, script fonts for prices.
- **Telehealth and pharmacy** · prescriber licensing, verifiable pharmacy credentials, price per visit · modern clinical / discreet · low · eligibility check before account creation, refund if the clinician says no · implying a guaranteed prescription.

## Commerce, food and hospitality
- **Fashion e-commerce** · fit data, returns, customer photos by size · gallery minimal / street colour · medium · first-load pop-ups, tiny grey prices, inconsistent product photography.
- **Skincare and beauty DTC** · full ingredient list, unretouched results · lab clean / botanical warm · medium · "chemical-free" claims, before/after without timeframe and consent.
- **Consumer products DTC** · demo video, specs with scale, warranty · object studio / playful utility · medium · resetting countdown timers, review widgets shifting the layout.
- **Restaurants and cafes** · an HTML menu with prices, accurate hours, real dishes · neighbourhood warmth / dark dining · low · PDF or image menus, autoplay sound, hours that disagree with Google.
- **Food and beverage brands** · store locator, nutrition and allergens, sourcing · pack-led pop / crafted provenance · medium · no way to find it in stores, unlawful health claims.
- **Hotels** · live rates, true room photos, direct-booking perks · coastal ease / urban boutique · medium · intro videos delaying the booking bar, resort fees revealed late.
- **Travel** · day-by-day itineraries, licensing and financial protection · bold adventure / curated journey · medium · no prices anywhere, itineraries in PDFs.
- **Luxury goods** · craft detail, provenance, advisor service · maison restraint / modern atelier · low · sale badges, crowded grids, thin grey text on ivory.

## Home and local services
- **HVAC** · licence numbers, published fees, named technicians · dispatch-ready (navy + safety orange) / comfort home · low · sticky Call (primary) + Book bar, online booking with arrival windows · stock technicians wearing other companies' logos, "24/7" that reaches voicemail, area pages with only the city name swapped.
- **Plumbing and electrical** · licences, published call-out fee · trusted trade / modern pro · low · a "What's the problem?" selector, text-to-book · cartoon plumbers and lightning graphics, 10-field emergency forms.
- **Roofing and construction** · local project gallery, manufacturer certifications, a physical address · built strong / craftsman heritage · low · inspection form with photo upload, financing with the lender named · renders shown as finished work, claims about insurance paying.
- **Landscaping** · portfolio, priced plans · fresh cut / garden designer · low · separate CTAs for maintenance and design-build, instant quote by address · imagery from another climate.
- **Pool builders** · deep gallery, build timeline, price ranges · resort blue / backyard architecture · medium · consultation booking capturing yard photos, budget and timeline · no price guidance, other builders' photos.
- **Pet services** · staff handling real pets, breed-based prices, safety policy · playful clean / caring clinical · medium · paw prints everywhere, wrong-breed stock pets.
- **Cleaning** · instant price, vetting, re-clean guarantee · crisp fresh / premium concierge · low · price gated behind contact details, nothing about who enters the home.
- **Auto** · certified technicians, a price menu, all-in pricing · garage grit / showroom clean · low · drip pricing that excludes mandatory fees (illegal in places such as California).

## Property, education, community, culture
- **Real estate** · recent local sales, licences, honest photos · local expert / prestige listing · medium · map search with saved alerts, mortgage calculator with assumptions shown · unlabelled virtual staging.
- **Property development** · track record, named team, renders labelled "indicative" · place-making story / investment grade · medium · interactive availability plan.
- **Architecture and interiors** · credited portfolio, registration, fee approach · gallery white / material warmth · low · cryptic navigation, tiny light-grey text.
- **Education** · accreditation, sourced outcomes, full cost · academic confident / bright learning · medium · giant mega menus without search, costs in PDFs.
- **Nonprofits** · registration numbers, financials, dignified stories · human documentary / bold movement · medium · a fast donation form with presets, monthly toggle and wallets, plus a separate calm "Get help" path · guilt imagery, unsourced impact numbers.
- **Government and civic** · official domain, plain language, accessibility statement · plain public / civic warmth · low · one thing per page, check-your-answers, save and return · organising by department instead of task; custom components when a national design system exists.
- **Events and weddings** · real event photos, priced packages, availability · romantic editorial / festival energy · high · script fonts for dates, date and city missing from the first screen.
- **Agencies and portfolios** · case studies that show thinking · type-led bold / quiet gallery · high · preloaders delaying the work, custom cursors hiding the pointer.
- **Media and publishing** · bylines, dates, corrections, labelled sponsorship · broadsheet / digital native · low · articles 18 to 20 px at 60 to 75 characters, reserved ad slots (no layout shift) · stacked pop-ups.
- **Gaming, music, film** · real gameplay, tour dates, full galleries · immersive / clean · high · huge hero video on mobile, neon text below contrast, stale dates.
